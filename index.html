The Library of Forgotten Stars

An AI-assisted fantasy mystery

Meet Nila

Nila is an eighteen-year-old girl who discovers a secret library beneath an abandoned clock tower. Its magical books contain forgotten memories, and mysterious stars are disappearing from the night sky.

Begin the Mystery

At midnight, every clock in the town stopped. Nila entered the hidden library and found a silver book with her name on its cover. Should she open it?

Created for CAPSTONE 67 — AI Storytelling and Creative Writing Lab

Human creativity guided the story; AI supported brainstorming and editing.
